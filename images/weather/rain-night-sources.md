# Rain and night background sources

The shipped files are photographs downloaded from Unsplash. Both final files are **3840 × 2160 pixels**. They were cropped and downsampled by the image host from larger originals, never upscaled. Original dimensions and final dimensions were verified with System.Drawing on 2026-09-13.

| Asset | Photographer | Original pixels | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rainy-4k.jpg` | Steve Gribble | 10748 × 8061 | [Dark storm clouds with heavy rain over a flat landscape](https://unsplash.com/photos/dark-storm-clouds-with-heavy-rain-over-a-flat-landscape-H9nT7mYrtlM) |
| `night-4k.jpg` | Ashwini Chaudhary(Monty) | 5627 × 4000 | [A full moon is seen above a mountain range](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-full-moon-is-seen-above-a-mountain-range-sVHVffpeklI) |

Both source pages label the photographs free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), checked on 2026-09-13. The license allows downloading and using images in commercial and noncommercial projects. These photos are used as weather app backgrounds.
