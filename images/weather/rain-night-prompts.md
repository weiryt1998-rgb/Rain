# Rejected generated rain and night candidates

Built-in image_gen mode was used, one call for each asset. Both outputs were 1672 × 941 pixels despite explicit UHD size requests, so they are **not shipped or referenced by the app**. Native 4K photographs were sourced instead; see `rain-night-sources.md`.

## Rain candidate prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: production weather dashboard full-screen background photo, landscape exactly 3840x2160 pixels (UHD 4K), 16:9.
Primary request: a distinctly rainy monsoon scene over lush Thai plains and distant low green hills. Unmistakable visible falling rain streaks across the scene and fine rain mist at the horizon. Vast layered gray-blue monsoon clouds dominate roughly the top 80 percent of the composition; low hazy rolling hills and tropical fields occupy the bottom.
Style/medium: extremely sharp natural landscape photography with finely detailed clouds and authentic rain, realistic atmosphere, no illustration.
Composition/framing: wide tranquil panorama, open spacious central sky suitable behind glass weather panels; keep landscape meaningful under a centered mobile cover crop.
Lighting/mood: soft dim rainy daylight, calm, luminous gray-blue clouds, natural muted green land.
Constraints: exactly 3840 by 2160 pixels, crisp real texture without oversharpening; no lightning, no visible sun, no buildings, no people, no text, no logos, no watermark, no UI.

## Night candidate prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: production weather dashboard full-screen background photo, exactly 3840x2160 pixels, UHD 4K landscape, 16:9.
Primary request: a tranquil clear night over lush Thai plains and distant low hills, beneath a deep midnight blue sky with a recognizable softly glowing moon in the upper right portion, modest natural scale, and gentle scattered stars. The moonlit sky fills roughly the upper 80 percent. Low dark forested hills and tropical fields form the bottom fifth.
Style/medium: extremely sharp natural landscape night photography, realistic fine lunar surface detail, believable atmospheric perspective and dim moonlit land texture.
Composition/framing: wide panorama with uncluttered central sky suitable behind glass weather panels and center mobile cover crop; peaceful open negative space.
Lighting/mood: calm moonlight, midnight cobalt blue, dim muted green hills, subtle soft glow close to the moon.
Constraints: render exactly 3840 by 2160 pixels, crisp genuine details; no science fiction, no exaggerated milky way, no giant moon, no rain, no storm, no buildings, no people, no text, no logos, no watermark, no UI.
